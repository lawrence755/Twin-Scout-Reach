Continuous Automation Loop

Stopped

On a timer, cycles through: transferring new Good Flip leads into the Outreach Review tab, pulling in your "Ready for Automated Outreach? = Yes" decisions, REI BlackBook enrich/notes/replies/send, email send, and (if Voice Automation is on) Google Voice. Live-Sending Switches

Written straight to .env -- takes effect on the next job run, no restart needed
Enable Email Sending Master switch for sending approved outreach emails via Gmail.
Enable Voice Automation Allows Google Voice preparation/automation to run at all.
Auto-send Google Voice Texts Clicks Send in Google Voice with no per-message confirmation.
Auto-send REI BlackBook Texts Clicks Send Text in REI BlackBook's Chat tab with no per-message confirmation.

This is the real switch the scripts check at runtime -- separate from, and in addition to, the "I understand" checkbox on the Automation tab. Both must be on for an auto-send job to actually send anything; with either off, it only prints a dry-run list of what would send.
